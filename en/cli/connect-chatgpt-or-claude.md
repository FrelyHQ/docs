# How do I connect ChatGPT or Claude to my computer?

First run `frely link on` on the computer; it prints the MCP URL. FrelyMCP is one address for your whole account: https://mcp.frely.cloud/mcp. In ChatGPT or Claude add a custom connector with that URL, sign in to Frely and approve the client. To check, ask the client to list the top-level names in your workspace.

Related: [FrelyMCP endpoint](https://mcp.frely.cloud/mcp) · [Install page](https://cli.frely.cloud/#install) · [README](https://github.com/FrelyHQ/frely-cli/blob/main/README.md)
