# Creating and selling on Frely

## What can I publish?
<!-- id: what-sell -->

A Prompt, Skill, Agent or workflow that you have the right to publish. Define its task, inputs, outputs and limits. What you sell is access to the capability, and your core prompts, code and workflows are not handed to buyers.

Related: [Pricing](https://frely.cloud/pricing/) · [Security and data](https://frely.cloud/security/)

## What is the difference between a hosted Agent and a download-install item?
<!-- id: hosted-vs-install -->

A hosted Agent runs on Frely and buyers use it through Frely, so your private content stays with you. A download-install Prompt or Skill delivers its files to the buyer's own device; the free part is visible to everyone who installs it, and Frely cannot recall a copy already installed. Content that must stay private should be published as a hosted Agent on a paid Creator plan.

Related: [Terms of Service](https://frely.cloud/terms/)

API tools: `skills.list`, `agents.list`

Related: [Pricing](https://frely.cloud/pricing/)

API tools: `creator.subscription`

## I have no publishing or product entry.
<!-- id: cannot-publish -->

Signing in alone does not grant publishing. Check that your plan is active and that you meet the publishing eligibility and commercial terms.

Related: [Terms of Service](https://frely.cloud/terms/)

## Is sales revenue the same as profit or a withdrawable balance?
<!-- id: revenue -->

No. Creators must account for model and service running costs, and revenue is not automatically profit or a balance you can withdraw. Settlement follows the service terms.

Related: [Terms of Service](https://frely.cloud/terms/)

## What rights do I need?
<!-- id: rights -->

You must hold the rights to distribute the files and content you publish and name the original source where needed. Content that infringes others' rights is not allowed.

Related: [Terms of Service](https://frely.cloud/terms/)
